Privacy Policy for Jigsaw Quest

Effective Date: July 29, 2026 | Package Name: com.iraps.jigsawquest

Summary: Your privacy is important to us. Jigsaw Quest does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Camera and Photo permissions are used exclusively on your local device to generate custom puzzle boards. Advertisements are served via Google AdMob in accordance with Google's Advertising policies.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jigsaw Quest ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download, install, and play our mobile application Jigsaw Quest (the "App").

2. Information Collection and Storage

We believe in data minimization and user privacy. The App is designed so that you can enjoy playing without providing any personal identity details.

A. Personal Data

We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials. No account creation is required to use the App.

B. Device Permissions (Camera & Photo Gallery)

To enable the feature of converting your personal photos into custom jigsaw puzzles, the App requests permission to access your device's Camera and Photo Library / Storage:

  • Camera Access: Used only when you choose to take a live photo within the App to turn into a puzzle.
  • Photo Access: Used only when you select an existing image from your device gallery to turn into a puzzle.

Important Note: Photos accessed or captured by the App are processed entirely locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, host, or share your photos or camera captures on any server.

C. Local Game Data

Your puzzle progress, high scores, unlocked themes, and app settings are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The App integrates third-party SDKs to serve advertisements and support app performance. These third parties may collect and process certain non-personal device information automatically.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to show ads within the App. Google AdMob may collect and use data such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID) or IDFA
  • IP address and approximate coarse location
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App interaction metrics and ad view data

AdMob uses this data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, combat ad fraud, and provide aggregated reporting. For more details on how Google handles your data, please review:

Google Privacy Policy • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your device data and permissions:

  • Revoke Permissions: You can grant or revoke Camera and Photo Gallery permissions at any time via your device's System Settings (Settings > Apps > Jigsaw Quest > Permissions).
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or limit your Advertising ID via your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Delete Game Data: Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored game data and preferences from your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
