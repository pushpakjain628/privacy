## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Jigsaw Quest** ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download, install, and play our mobile application Jigsaw Quest (the "App").

## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Personal Data
We do **not** collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials. No account creation or registration is required to use the App.

### B. Device Permissions (Camera & Photo Gallery)
To enable the feature of converting your personal photos into custom jigsaw puzzles, the App requests permission to access your device's **Camera** and **Photo Library / Storage**:
- **Camera Access:** Used only when you choose to take a live photo within the App to turn into a puzzle.
- **Photo Access:** Used only when you select an existing image from your device gallery to turn into a puzzle.

*Note:* Photos accessed or captured by the App are processed **entirely locally on your device**. We do not transmit, upload, host, or share your photos or camera captures on any external server.

### C. Local Game Data
Your puzzle progress, high scores, unlocked themes, and app settings are saved locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements
The App integrates third-party SDKs to serve advertisements and support app performance. These third parties may collect and process certain non-personal device information automatically.

### Google AdMob
We use **Google AdMob** to show ads within the App. Google AdMob may collect and use data such as:
- Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID) or IDFA
- IP address and coarse location
- Device information (model, OS version, screen resolution)
- App interaction metrics and ad view data

AdMob uses this data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, combat ad fraud, and provide aggregated reporting. For more details, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

## 5. Your Rights and Controls
You have full control over your device data and permissions:
- **Revoke Permissions:** You can grant or revoke Camera and Photo Gallery permissions at any time via your device's System Settings (`Settings > Apps > Jigsaw Quest > Permissions`).
- **Opt-Out of Personalized Ads:** You can reset or limit your Advertising ID via your device settings (`Android: Settings > Google > Ads`; `iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking`).
- **Delete Game Data:** Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored game data and preferences from your device.
